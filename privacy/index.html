Information we collect

When you call us, we collect your phone number, your address and a description of the problem so we can send a plumber. When you email us, we collect your email address and the content of your message. This site does not collect information through forms.

Our web server and hosting provider keep standard technical logs, such as the pages requested and the type of browser. We use that information to keep the site running and secure.

Call recording notice

Calls to our phone line may be recorded or monitored for quality, training and to keep an accurate record of what was agreed. If you continue the call after hearing the notice, you agree to the recording. If you do not want a call recorded, tell us at the start of the call or contact us by email instead.

How we use information

We use your information to answer your call, schedule and perform service, send a price and invoice, answer questions and meet legal and tax requirements. We do not sell your personal information.

Third party services

This site loads fonts from Google Fonts and embeds maps from Google Maps. Those services may receive your IP address and browser details when the page loads, under their own privacy policies. Phone calls pass through our telephone provider.

Sharing

We share information only with people who need it to do the work, such as the plumber who visits you, and with service providers who help us run the business. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

Retention and security

We keep job records for as long as we need them for service, warranty, tax and legal purposes. We use reasonable steps to protect information, but no method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure.

Your choices and California rights

You can ask what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it or ask us to delete it, subject to legal limits. Contact us at contact@proplumbingcamarillo.com or (805) 249-7135. We will respond as the law requires.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy and will change the date when we do. Questions can go to Camarillo Pro Plumbing, 2400 Las Posas Road, Camarillo, CA 93010, or contact@proplumbingcamarillo.com. This policy was last updated on November 14, 2026.
