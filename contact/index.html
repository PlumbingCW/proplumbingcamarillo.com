How to reach us

Call (805) 249-7135 at any hour. For an emergency, close the nearest shutoff first, then call and tell us what is happening. For a planned job or a question, the phone is still the quickest, and our plumbers can usually answer the first questions on the call.

Our email is contact@proplumbingcamarillo.com. We use the phone for urgent matters because email can wait. There is no form on this site, so we do not ask you to type in details you would rather say out loud.

Our address and hours

Camarillo Pro Plumbing, 2400 Las Posas Road, Camarillo, CA 93010. The dispatch line is open 24 hours a day, seven days a week. The office address is for mail and for orientation on the map, not a walk-in counter, so call before you visit.

What to have ready when you call

It helps to know what you see, hear and smell, where the problem is in the house and when it started. If you have a leak, note the meter reading. If a drain is backed up, tell us which fixtures are affected. If a water heater is failing, share the age and fuel type. See drain cleaning and water heater repair for what we ask on those calls.

For service outside Camarillo, see our Oxnard page and our service areas page. If you are not sure we reach your address, call and ask.
