How to choose the right service

Start with what is happening. If water is spreading, sewage is backing up or you have no hot water, call the 24/7 line and describe it. We will point you to the right service and tell you the first step. If the problem is slow or planned, such as a remodel or a water heater that is aging but working, you can choose a visit that fits your schedule.

What we ask before we price a job

We ask about the age of the house, what you see and hear and where the fixture or line sits. The answers shape the price, and we give a clear price before work starts. Camarillo homes range from 1960s ranch houses to newer tract homes, so the equipment under the sink and in the garage varies a lot.

The City of Camarillo reports well water hardness averaging 217.6 ppm. That matters for water heaters, faucets and old pipe, and it is part of why we pay attention to scale during repairs. For a closer look at the city report, see our water softener and filtration page.
