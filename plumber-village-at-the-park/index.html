About Village at the Park

Village at the Park is a planned neighborhood that the City of Camarillo approved under a specific plan in October 2001, according to a local real estate source. The same source says the first homes were built around 2005 and describes about 644 homes. The Camarillo Family YMCA is at 3111 Village at the Park Drive, and street addresses use ZIP code 93012.

The same source says single-family homes there are not governed by a mandatory association and that internal streets were dedicated to the city. We could not confirm that on a city page, so check your own deed and tax records. Because these homes are newer, we do not assume any pipe problem. The useful topics are upkeep and water.

Maintenance topics for newer homes

This is a maintenance angle for newer houses, not a claim about common defects.

Water heater care

A heater a decade or more old benefits from a flush and a rod check. See water heater repair.

Tankless descaling

Tankless units in hard water need descaling on a schedule. See tankless water heater.

Scale on fixtures

White crust on faucets and showerheads is a hard water sign. See water softener and filtration.

Leak checks

A meter that moves with everything off, or a damp wall. See leak detection.

Planned upgrades

New fixtures, a laundry line or a bathroom change. See plumbing installation.

Water numbers worth knowing

The City of Camarillo's 2021 water report says about 26 percent of supply comes from city wells and about 74 percent from Calleguas Municipal Water District. Well water hardness averaged 217.6 ppm, with a range of 101 to 350 ppm. Those are citywide numbers, not specific to this neighborhood.

Ask your water provider which source serves your address, then decide whether treatment or a regular heater flush makes sense. We can explain the options on the phone.

Find the water heater, the main shutoff and any pressure regulator in your home and learn what each one looks like. If the pressure seems too high or too low, a simple hose-bib gauge gives you a number to tell us when you call. That reading lets us talk through the likely causes before anyone drives out.
