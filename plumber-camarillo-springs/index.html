About Camarillo Springs

Community sources describe Camarillo Springs as a neighborhood off Camarillo Springs Road, near US Route 101 and the foothills of the Santa Monica Mountains. It surrounds the east end of the public Camarillo Springs Golf Course and was built in phases, with single-family homes, condos, townhouses, duplexes and mobile homes in different sections.

Street addresses use Camarillo ZIP code 93012. We could not find a city document that sets exact boundaries, so we describe it by Camarillo Springs Road and the golf course rather than by a boundary line. A local real estate blog also describes The Springs, a gated section for residents 55 and older with 259 single-family homes. Treat that as a listing description, not an official record.

Why each phase matters for plumbing

This is how a mixed-housing neighborhood changes the first questions. It is not a claim that any of these problems is common here.

Different associations

Each phase has its own owners' association and rules. Ask what the association handles before you call.

Shared walls and lines

In condos, townhouses and duplexes, a leak can reach a neighbor. Tell management early.

Garage and closet water heaters

Location and venting rules vary by building. See water heater replacement and water heater installation.

Landscape and hose bibs

Outdoor faucets and irrigation tie-ins can leak behind plantings. See leak detection.

Water facts that apply

The City of Camarillo's 2021 water report says about 26 percent of the supply comes from city wells and about 74 percent from Calleguas Municipal Water District. Well water averaged 217.6 ppm of hardness, with a range of 101 to 350 ppm. Those are citywide numbers, and your tap may differ.

Scale in a water heater or on a showerhead is a sign worth acting on. Our tankless water heater page covers descaling, and our water softener and filtration page covers treatment options.
