When a failed tank needs replacing right away

Some water heaters die quietly with a cold shower. Others fail with a flood. If the bottom of the tank is wet or the seam is weeping, the steel has corroded through and no repair will hold. At that point the job is to limit water damage tonight and replace the unit as soon as a plumber can.

Closing the cold water valve above the heater stops the tank from refilling, which limits how much water can escape. If any part of the situation involves gas odor, leave the building and call the gas utility or 911 first.

Replacement options for a Camarillo home

Most homes here have a 40 or 50 gallon tank in a garage or utility closet. A like-for-like swap is the quickest option and usually the lowest cost. Some homeowners use the failure as a reason to change the setup, which opens up three real choices.

A new tank of the same fuel type is the baseline. A tankless unit gives continuous hot water and frees floor space, but it needs the right gas line size and venting, which is covered on our tankless water heater page.

A heat pump or hybrid model uses much less electricity, though it needs space and airflow. We explain the tradeoffs on site so you choose with the numbers in front of you.

Tank, tankless and hybrid compared

The choice depends on your house, not on a brochure. Here is the short version we walk through on site.

TypeBest fitWatch for
Standard tankHouseholds that want the lowest upfront cost and a fast swapStandby heat loss and a service life of roughly 8 to 12 years.
TanklessLarger families, limited floor space, long periods of useHigher install cost, gas line and vent upgrades, scale in hard water.
Hybrid heat pumpElectric homes with room around the unitNeeds clearance and airflow, and makes noise like a small fan.

Permits, bracing and what to ask

The City of Camarillo Building and Safety Division lists water heater replacements among the permits that can be applied for online. The office number is (805) 388-5395, and inspection requests go through the city system. Permit rules can change, so ask the city or your plumber what applies to your address before the work begins.

California is earthquake country, and installers here typically strap or brace water heaters so they stay upright in a shake. Ask that the bracing, a drain pan where the location calls for one, and a proper discharge line for the relief valve be part of the quote. These items are small, and they are what protect a garage when something goes wrong.

Hard water changes how long a new heater lasts

The City of Camarillo's water quality report puts its well water hardness at 217.6 ppm on average, with samples from 101 to 350 ppm. That is well above the level where scale builds on heating elements and tank bottoms. A new heater in hard water benefits from an annual flush, an anode rod check and an expansion tank where the home has a closed plumbing system.

If hard water has already shortened the life of your old unit, pricing a water softener at the same time is sensible. We would rather you decide that before installation than after the next failure.
