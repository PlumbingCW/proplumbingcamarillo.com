About Mission Oaks

Mission Oaks is a named area in Camarillo's General Plan and a name residents use for the neighborhoods around Mission Oaks Boulevard and Santa Rosa Road. A local real estate blog describes shopping centers at that intersection, Mission Oaks Park and Heritage Park, and nearby streets such as Verdugo Way and Woodcreek. Addresses use ZIP code 93012.

A neighborhood data service reports that most homes there were built from 1970 to 1999, with a smaller share since 2000, and that most are medium-sized single-family homes. We could not find an official boundary, so we describe the area by its anchor streets. Treat the building dates as a general guide, not a record for your address.

Plumbing needs for single-family homes

These are common single-family service calls. They are not a claim that Mission Oaks homes fail more often than homes elsewhere.

Water heater age and condition

Check the date on the tank label, listen for popping and look for rust at the base. See water heater repair and water heater replacement.

Slow or backing-up drains

One slow fixture usually means a local clog. Several fixtures at once point to the main line. See drain cleaning and sewer line cleaning.

Sewer lateral questions

In a house with a front yard tree, a camera inspection shows what is in the line. See sewer camera inspection.

Water line and pressure

A drop in pressure or a wet patch near the meter can mean a service line problem. See water line repair.

Kitchen and bath fixtures

Dripping faucets, running toilets and jammed disposals. See faucet repair and garbage disposal repair.

Who owns the sewer lateral

The Camarillo Sanitary District operations code says the property owner maintains the side sewer, including the lateral, and it calls for a cleanout. Sewer service differs by address in and around Camarillo, so confirm which agency serves yours before you assume the public main is at fault.

If you are unsure whether a backup sits in your pipe or the public line, a camera inspection gives you video to share with the agency. If the lateral is damaged, sewer line repair covers spot repairs, lining and replacement.

A short checklist for Mission Oaks homeowners

Five checks take about ten minutes.

  • Know where your main water shutoff is and test it twice a year.
  • Find your sewer cleanout and keep the cap clear of plants.
  • Check the water heater label for its age.
  • Look for slow drains in more than one fixture.
  • Write down your meter reading with all fixtures off to check for leaks.

Hard water and the city's numbers

The City of Camarillo's 2021 report puts well water hardness at an average of 217.6 ppm. That is a citywide number. Scale builds up in water heaters and on faucets, so a flush and a rod check help. See water softener and filtration for treatment options.
