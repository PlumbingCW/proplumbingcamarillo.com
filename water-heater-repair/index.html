Cold water or a puddle: what counts as urgent

A water heater with no hot water is uncomfortable. A water heater that is leaking is urgent, because a tank leak can empty the tank into a garage or closet and soak drywall. Water coming from the top of the tank at a fitting is often repairable. Water coming from the bottom or the tank seam usually means the steel has failed, and the unit needs replacement instead of repair.

If you smell gas, leave the house and call the gas utility or 911 before you call a plumber. Do not use any switch or flame. For a leak with no gas smell, close the cold supply valve above the heater, which stops more water entering the tank, and call us at any hour.

Symptoms and what they usually mean

Callers describe water heater trouble in a few common ways. Each one narrows the cause.

No hot water at all

On a gas unit, a pilot that will not stay lit often points to a worn thermocouple or a draft problem. On an electric unit, a tripped breaker, a failed upper element or a bad thermostat are the usual causes.

Hot water runs out fast

A failed lower element on an electric tank or a tank full of sediment cuts the usable supply. Camarillo hard water makes sediment buildup common.

Rumbling or popping noises

Sediment at the bottom traps steam bubbles under it. Flushing can help early on, and a heavily scaled tank may be past saving.

Rusty or brown hot water

If only the hot side runs rusty, the tank lining or anode rod has likely given out. Cold water that is also rusty points to the supply pipes instead.

Water on the floor

Check whether it comes from a pipe fitting, the relief valve discharge or the tank body. Each has a different repair path.

Relief valve dripping

A dripping temperature and pressure relief valve can signal excess pressure or an overheated tank. It should be checked, never capped.

How we diagnose the problem

We start with the fuel and power: gas supply and pilot or igniter on a gas unit, breaker and element tests on an electric one. Then we check the thermostat, the relief valve, the dip tube, the anode rod and the fittings for leaks.

For tankless problems we read the error code and check the water flow and the heat exchanger. Our tankless water heater service page covers those units in more detail.

We tell you what we find before we repair anything. A part that costs little, such as a thermocouple or a thermostat, is often the right fix. When the tank itself has failed, we say so plainly and show you the comparison between repair and replacement.

Hard water and your water heater in Camarillo

The City of Camarillo's annual water quality report lists hardness in parts per million. Its own well water averaged 217.6 ppm, with a range of 101 to 350. Calleguas Municipal Water District water, which makes up the larger share of the city supply, was reported at 122 ppm from the Jensen plant, and locally stored surface water treated by Calleguas at 272 ppm.

Laboratories commonly call anything over 180 ppm very hard. At that level, minerals settle on elements and at the bottom of the tank.

That scale insulates the heating surface, so the heater works harder and fails sooner. A tank flush once a year and an anode rod check every few years are cheap protection. If your elements have failed more than once, a water softener or scale control, covered on our water softener and filtration page, may be worth pricing alongside the next repair.

Before the plumber arrives

These steps limit damage and give us what we need to quote correctly.

  • Turn off the cold water valve above the heater if there is any leak.
  • Set the gas control to off or switch off the breaker if the unit is electric.
  • Clear items from around the heater so the plumber has room to work.
  • Note the age on the data plate, which includes the serial number and the model.
  • Do not remove the relief valve or plug its discharge pipe.

What affects the cost of a water heater repair

We quote before we start. The usual drivers are:

The partA thermocouple, element or thermostat sits at the low end, and a gas control valve costs more.
Fuel typeGas units add venting and combustion checks that electric units do not.
Age and accessOlder units and tight closets take more time and sometimes more parts.
TimingAfter hours and weekend calls are priced differently from a scheduled visit.

Homeowners insurance usually covers sudden water damage but not the heater that failed. Ask your insurer how it treats a leaking tank. For your number, call (805) 249-7135.

Safety standards behind a heater repair

Water heater repairs follow the manufacturer's service instructions and the California Plumbing Code (CPC), which is based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC) for relief valves and venting. A relief valve that drips, a flue that draws poorly or a gas smell are safety items, not comfort items, and we treat them that way. Call the gas utility or 911 first if you smell gas.
