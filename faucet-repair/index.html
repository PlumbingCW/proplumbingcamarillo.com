A fixture that will not shut off

Every fixture should have its own shutoff valve, called an angle stop under a sink or a stop behind a toilet. When that valve is old, it may not close, or it may snap off when turned. If water is flowing and the stop will not close, shut off the main valve at the house. That is the valve to know before an emergency.

A supply line that bursts or a faucet body that cracks needs prompt attention. Place a towel and a bucket and keep water off the cabinet floor, because a wet cabinet base swells and molds. Once the water is off, we replace the line, the valve or the faucet and test everything under the sink for leaks.

Problems we repair

Fixtures fail in predictable ways.

Dripping faucet

A worn cartridge, washer or seat. A steady drip wastes water and stains the basin.

Low flow at the spout

Scale clogs the aerator or the cartridge. This is a common issue in hard water.

Handle that leaks at the base

Worn O-rings or a loose packing nut.

Leak under the sink

A loose slip nut, a failing supply line or a cracked trap.

Stiff or frozen stop valve

Old valves seize with age. Replacing them makes the next repair simple.

Slow sink drain

A clog in the trap or the line, handled through drain cleaning.

Repair or replace the faucet

A faucet with a worn cartridge is a quick repair. A faucet with a corroded body, a loose mount or a finish that has worn away is better replaced. Replacement lets you choose a model that fits your sink and your hand, and the labor cost is similar.

We carry common cartridges and supply lines on the truck. If a part is not available, we tell you and quote a replacement. For a remodel with a new sink, see plumbing installation.

Hard water and fixtures in Camarillo

The City of Camarillo reports well water hardness averaging 217.6 ppm, with a range of 101 to 350 ppm. Minerals leave white crust at the aerator and in the cartridge, and they harden the rubber seals over time. That is why we see low flow and stiff handles earlier here than in soft water areas.

A vinegar soak removes scale from an aerator, which is a simple fix between visits. If scale returns every few months, a softener may be worth pricing, and our softener and filtration page explains the options.

What to do before we arrive

These steps help the plumber work quickly.

  • Close the shutoff valve under the sink by turning it clockwise.
  • If it will not close, close the main water valve at the house.
  • Clear the cabinet and place towels and a bucket.
  • Note the brand on the faucet if it is visible.
  • Take a photo of the leak or the damaged part.

Parts, listings and the plumbing code

Faucets, supply lines and drains fall under the California Plumbing Code (CPC), which is based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC). We replace parts with ones that carry a recognized listing mark, because a part that is not listed for potable water can fail or leave a taste.

If a repair shows that a shutoff valve or a drain is out of date, we tell you which part to replace and why.
