Who we are

We are a local plumbing service centered on Camarillo. Our office is at 2400 Las Posas Road, Camarillo, CA 93010, and our phone line is open at all hours. We serve Camarillo ZIP codes 93010 and 93012 and nearby towns, including Oxnard, Moorpark, Newbury Park and Somis.

We keep the company simple on purpose. You call, you describe the problem, and we tell you the next step. We do not publish counts of jobs or years in business that we cannot prove on this page, and we do not use reviews or awards we cannot show. What we can show is the work, the sources we use and the way we explain a job.

How we work

Every job starts with a conversation about what you see and hear. We give first steps to limit damage, then send a plumber. You get a clear price before work starts, and the plumber explains the repair in plain terms. We book same-day appointments when we can, and we do not promise arrival times we cannot keep.

We take emergency calls at any hour. For planned work, such as plumbing installation or repiping, we walk the job, write the scope and explain permits. Our services page lists everything we do.

Why we use local sources

Plumbing facts vary by place. The City of Camarillo reports well water hardness averaging 217.6 ppm, and that changes how we think about water heaters and fixtures. The Camarillo Sanitary District operations code says the owner maintains the side sewer, including the lateral, and that changes how we explain a sewer repair.

We cite those sources on our pages so you can check them. When we are not sure of a rule, we say so and point you to the office that decides, such as the City of Camarillo Building and Safety Division at (805) 388-5395 or the Water Division at (805) 388-5373.

Licensing and permits

California requires a contractor license for projects of $1,000 or more, and the Contractors State License Board lets you check any license at cslb.ca.gov or 800-321-2752. We encourage you to check every plumber you hire. We also tell you which permits apply to your job and who pulls them.

What we leave to others

We repair pipes and fixtures. We do not do water extraction, mold work or drywall finishing, and we say so on the phone. For a burst pipe, see burst pipe repair, where we explain what to do about the water damage and how to document it for your insurer.

What a visit looks like

When the plumber arrives, they listen first, look at the problem and test before they quote. They explain what they found in plain words, show you the failed part where they can and describe the options. You decide what happens next. If the best answer is a small repair, we say so. If the best answer is a replacement, we explain why and what it involves.

After the work, the plumber tests the repair, cleans the area and walks you through what was done. You get an invoice that matches the price you agreed to, and you get guidance on what to watch for. If you have a question later, call the same number.

How we think about pricing

We give a clear price before work starts. A price depends on the problem, the access, the parts and the time of day, and we explain each of those on the phone or at the door. We do not promise a price we cannot keep. If the job turns out to be different from what we saw, we stop, explain and agree on the change before we continue.

You can see how we describe the factors on each service page, such as water heater replacement and sewer line repair. If you want to compare a quote from another company, ask both companies to put the scope in writing and compare the same items.

Sources we rely on

We rely on named sources, including the California Plumbing Code (CPC), which is based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC), the City of Camarillo 2021 water quality report, the Camarillo Sanitary District operations code and the California Contractors State License Board. According to those sources, hardness averages 217.6 ppm, owners maintain side sewers and licenses are required for projects of $1,000 or more.
